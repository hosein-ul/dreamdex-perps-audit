Perps on Testnet

Perpetual futures (perps) are live on the dreamDEX testnet for paper trading. A perp is a leveraged long or short on an asset's price with no expiry date. Funding payments between longs and shorts keep it tracking the asset instead.

Testnet perps run in the DreamDEX region of app.testnet.dreamdex.io. Everything here uses test funds: nothing you win or lose is real money.

Perps are on the testnet only. There are no perp markets on mainnet yet.

Get started

  1. Sign in at app.testnet.dreamdex.io. On testnet your wallets live in DreamDEX, the region the perp markets are in.
  2. Get paper USDso. Join Perps Arena, the paper-trading competition, to receive 1,000 paper USDso to trade with. See Paper USDso for what you can do with it.
  3. Move margin into Perps if you hold USDso in your spot wallet: open Perps ⇆ Spot on the perps page and move it from Spot wallet to Perps account. Perp orders only use margin that is already in the Perps account.
  4. Place an order. Pick a market, set your size and leverage, and choose a market or limit order. See Orders.

Want a bot to trade for you? Link a Bot lets one trade a wallet of yours without being able to withdraw.

Costs

Maker fee0
Taker fee0
FundingPaid between longs and shorts every hour, never to the protocol. See Funding
Liquidation feeUp to 1% of the notional closed, only if you are liquidated. See Margin & Liquidations
TP and SL0.15 STT held per pending TP or SL, refunded when you cancel it. See Orders
